About Ramen

Ramen is my favourite meal: springy noodles in a rich, steaming broth, finished with a handful of toppings. This page is my little cooking corner where I collect what makes a great bowl.

Three Steps to a Good Bowl

1. Broth

Simmer bones, garlic and ginger slowly for a deep flavour.

2. Noodles

Boil fresh noodles for just a couple of minutes, then drain.

3. Toppings

Arrange the toppings on top and serve while it is hot.
