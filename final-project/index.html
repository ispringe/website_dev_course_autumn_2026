Why ride

More than a bike ride

Mountain biking takes you away from traffic and into nature. Every trail is a small puzzle of speed, balance and choosing the right line.

  1. Get outside

    Forests, hills and lakeside paths become your playground in every season.

  2. Build strength

    Climbing and descending train your legs, core and heart while it still feels like play.

  3. Clear your head

    On a technical trail you can only think about the next metre, which is a great break from screens.

  4. Ride together

    Group rides are social. Riders share tips, wait for each other and celebrate small wins.

Facts

Mountain biking in numbers

  • 1970s Mountain biking was born on the hills of Marin County, California.
  • 1996 Cross-country mountain biking became an Olympic sport in Atlanta.
  • 29" The most common wheel size on modern trail bikes.
  • 4 Main riding styles: cross-country, trail, enduro and downhill.
