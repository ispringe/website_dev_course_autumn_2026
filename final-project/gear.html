Essential kit

Pack before you ride

You do not need expensive gear to start. These items keep you safe and get you home if something goes wrong.

  • A helmet that fits well. Always.
  • Gloves for grip and to protect your hands in a fall.
  • Water and a snack for rides longer than an hour.
  • Spare inner tube, tyre levers and a mini pump.
  • A multi-tool to tighten loose bolts.
  • A charged phone and a map of the area.
FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an expensive bike to start?

No. A well-maintained second-hand hardtail is a great first bike. Spend money on a good helmet first and upgrade the bike when you know what kind of riding you enjoy.

Which wheel size should I choose?

Most adult bikes now use 29 inch wheels, which roll easily over roots and rocks. Smaller 27.5 inch wheels feel more playful and can suit shorter riders. Some bikes mix both.

Flat pedals or clipless pedals?

Start with flat pedals. They let you put a foot down quickly and teach good technique. Many cross-country riders later switch to clipless pedals for more pedalling power.

What tyre pressure should I use?

Lower pressure gives more grip but risks pinch flats. A common starting point is about 1.6 bar at the front and 1.8 bar at the rear, then adjust for your weight and the trail.

How often should I service my bike?

Clean and lube the chain after muddy rides and check the brakes and tyres before every ride. A full service once or twice a year keeps the suspension and drivetrain working well.
