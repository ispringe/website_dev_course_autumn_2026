The project

Why this website exists

Dirt & Pine is my final project for the Website Development course at Laurea University of Applied Sciences. The goal was to design, build and publish a complete website of my own that also works well on mobile devices.

I chose mountain biking because it is a great way to explore nature and clear your mind, and I wanted to encourage other people to try it too.

  • Written by hand in HTML5 and CSS3, with one shared stylesheet
  • Every interactive feature works without JavaScript
  • Responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops
  • Checked with the W3C HTML and CSS validators
